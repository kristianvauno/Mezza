Privacy notice

Mezza collects the name, mobile number, email, and message you type on this site so we can answer a property inquiry, schedule a viewing, or discuss a listing. That is a legitimate and declared purpose under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

On this demonstration site, inquiries are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to a server. A live client deployment should replace that with a secured inbox or CRM, a retention period, and a named data-protection contact.

We do not sell inquiry lists. We may share your details with the property owner, a cooperating broker, a bank, or Pag-IBIG only as needed to complete the transaction you asked for.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, or withdraw consent for marketing messages, by writing to hello@mezza.ph. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Do not send copies of IDs, titles, or tax records through the public form. Bring those to a viewing or email them only after we confirm a secure channel.
