OFW · dual citizen · foreign spouse

Come home to a title that will hold.

General Santos has land, not a condo skyline. That means the Constitution, not a glossy brochure, decides whether you can buy.

You kept or reacquired Philippine citizenship

You buy land like any Filipino. Dual citizenship under RA 9225 is the cleanest path for a house-and-lot in City Heights or a lot in Bula.

You are a former natural-born Filipino

You may still buy land, but the area is capped (RA 8179 / BP 185). Urban residential land is commonly discussed at up to 1,000 sqm; rural residential at up to one hectare. Business use has higher caps. Bring your proof of former citizenship.

You are a foreign national

You cannot own the land. You may lease a house or lot, own a building in limited structures, or buy a condominium unit if the project stays within the 40% foreign cap. GenSan has few true condo projects — do not force a land deal into a dummy title.

We will not put a Filipino spouse, driver, or “business partner” on a TCT to park land for a foreigner. That is the fact pattern the Anti-Dummy Law was written to punish.
