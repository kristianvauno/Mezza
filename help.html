Help center

How Mezza works

Buyers

How do I search?

Use barangay and type, or type a phrase like “pet-friendly house in Lagao under 3M”. Sort by nearest to me if you allow location.

How do I contact an agent?

Request a viewing (email + text), WhatsApp, call, or send an in-site message. Agents read those in Inbox.

What does Verified mean?

The agent filled name, mobile, photo, and PRC number on their desk.

Agents

How do I become a certified manager?

Sign up, complete My profile (photo + PRC), then tick “Apply as Certified Mezza Manager”. Buyers will see a Certified tag on your public page.

Where do inquiries go?

Inbox on this device, plus email and SMS if the buyer used those buttons.

Trust & safety

Are listings platform-verified?

Mezza marks the agent when their desk is complete. Always walk the property and read the TCT yourself.

How do I report a problem?

Use the listing’s message form and write “REPORT” in the first line. This demo keeps reports in the agent inbox.
