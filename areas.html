Neighborhoods in General Santos

GenSan is a highly urbanized city of about 722,000 people on Sarangani Bay. The right barangay depends on whether you want a village gate, a shop-house, a farm, or the airport road.

How we talk about this city

Pioneer Avenue and Santiago Boulevard still organize downtown. SM, KCC, Robinsons, Gaisano, and Veranza pull daily traffic. The airport sits in Tambler. The bay sits under Bula and the older coastal puroks. Upland barangays such as Conel trade access for air and views.

We do not rank barangays as “posh” or “cheap.” We tell you who the listing is for, and what breaks a deal: unsigned titles, agricultural conversion, village restrictions, or a house that was not looked at after 8 June 2026.
